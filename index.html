Research
InfoSeeking Lab | Nov 2025 – Present
Supervisor: Prof. Chirag Shah; PhD Mentor: Kirandeep Kaur; University of Washington. Co-authored a position paper on proactive agents operating under epistemic incompleteness, developed the framework linking epistemic legitimacy with behavioral commitment, and synthesized prior work on proactive, mixed-initiative, and anticipatory systems to identify when agents should act, defer, downshift, or abstain.
Project Lead, Undergraduate Researcher | Sep 2025 – Present
Collaborator: Kirandeep Kaur, Xinye Luo Initiated and led PROS, a multimodal research project for optimizing scientific poster drafts in editable PPTX format. Built a poster-optimization pipeline combining structured PPTX representation, LLM-based content diagnosis, VLM-based visual diagnosis, and constraint-aware editing, designed execution and repair modules to preserve layout validity while improving content quality, visual hierarchy, and poster readability, and defined benchmark criteria for poster-specific deficiencies and optimization-oriented evaluation.
Project Lead, Undergraduate Researcher | Nov 2024 – May 2025
Supervisor: Prof. David P. Woodruff; Carnegie Mellon University. Initiated and led P-RAG, a research project on retrieval-augmented biomedical and multi-hop question answering; co-developed the method by integrating retrieval, LoRA-based parametric adaptation, selective chain-of-thought prompting, and data augmentation; designed and ran experiments on PubMedQA and 2WikiMultihopQA, achieving 93.33% F1 on PubMedQA and improving total score on 2WikiMultihopQA from 17.83 to 33.44; and co-first-authored the resulting peer-reviewed conference paper.
