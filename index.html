Xingda Lyu (David)

Hi! I'm an undergraduate researcher at the University of Washington in the Computational Minds and Machines Lab with Prof. Max Kleiman-Weiner. I was a summer research intern with Prof. Jiawei Han at UIUC, and I also collaborate with researchers in the UW NLP Group. Previously, I worked with Prof. Chirag Shah in the UW InfoSeeking Lab.

My research focuses on proactive and adaptive AI agents. I'm interested in how agents can recognize gaps in users' understanding, decide when and how to help, and improve through feedback and experience. My work spans human-AI collaboration, scientific discovery, and multimodal generation.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
