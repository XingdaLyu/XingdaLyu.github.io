Hello and welcome! I’m a junior at the University of Washington, pursuing double degrees in Statistics and Informatics: Data Science. I work on proactive and generative AI agents, human-centered decision support under epistemic uncertainty, and structured document intelligence.

I am supervised by Prof. Chirag Shah and Kirandeep Kaur at UW, and advised by Prof. Daqiang Zhang at Tongji University. My recent work centers on epistemically grounded proactivity, uncertainty-aware assistance, and constraint-preserving editing agents for scientific posters and other structured visual documents.
