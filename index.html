Hi! I’m an undergraduate at the University of Washington pursuing dual B.S. degrees in Statistics (Data Science Track) and Informatics (Honors). My research focuses on proactive AI agents, information retrieval, human-centered AI, and natural language processing, especially systems that help users reason about uncertainty, knowledge gaps, and under-specified information needs.

I work with Prof. Chirag Shah at the University of Washington InfoSeeking Lab.

Research
InfoSeeking Lab | Nov 2025 – Present
Supervisor: Prof. Chirag Shah; PhD Mentor: Kirandeep Kaur; University of Washington.
I conduct research on proactive AI agents that support users under uncertainty, incomplete context, and unarticulated information needs. My work includes an ICML 2026 position paper on epistemic and behavioral foundations for proactive agentic systems, along with empirical extensions through benchmark design, model-based experiments, and analysis of proactive agent behavior.
Research Project Lead, PROS | Sep 2025 – Present
Project Lead; University of Washington.
I initiated and lead PROS, a research project on proactive multimodal refinement of scientific posters using editable PPTX representations. The project studies poster improvement as a source-grounded visual editing workflow, where the system diagnoses layout and scientific communication deficiencies, applies staged repairs, and supports constraint-aware editing to improve layout validity, section balance, visual clarity, and paper-grounded communication.
Research Project Lead, Algorithms for Big Data Program | Nov 2024 – May 2025
Supervisor: Prof. David P. Woodruff; Project Lead; Carnegie Mellon University.
I initiated and led P-RAG, a research project on retrieval-augmented biomedical and multi-hop question answering. The work combined retrieval, LoRA-based parametric adaptation, selective chain-of-thought prompting, and data augmentation, with experiments on PubMedQA and 2WikiMultihopQA that led to a peer-reviewed conference paper.
