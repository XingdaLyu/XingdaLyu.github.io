Xingda Lyu (David)

Hi! I'm an undergraduate researcher in the Computational Minds and Machines Lab at the University of Washington, advised by Prof. Max Kleiman-Weiner.

I was a research intern with Prof. Jiawei Han in the UIUC Data Mining Group in summer 2026. I am also collaborating with researchers in the UW NLP Group. Previously, I was advised by Prof. Chirag Shah at the InfoSeeking Lab.

My research focuses on:

  • Human-AI collaboration: designing proactive, personalized agents that adapt to users' goals and preferences over long-term interactions.
  • Epistemic AI: modeling users' structural ignorance and knowledge gaps to guide when and how agents intervene.
  • AI for Science: building retrieval-augmented agents with adaptive memory for scientific reasoning.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
